Mac ’N Cheese & Baked Ziti

Oh the debates about Mac ‘N Cheese! For such a comfort food (and its close cousin, Baked Ziti), there is a tempest of opinions about how best to make this classic – for kids and adults.
On one point there is agreement, at least in our house: No Kraft instant Mac ‘N Cheese.
In one New York Times article, they talked about various recipes with “white sauces,” funky cheeses, Worcestershire sauce, bread crumbs, etc. But, in the end, the “fantasy of what the dish should be is nothing more than tender elbows of pasta suspended in pure molten cheddar, with a chewy, golden-brown crust of cheese on top.”
And, this recipe is especially easy, with uncooked elbow pasta!
Mac ‘N Cheese & Baked Ziti
  Mac ‘N Cheese
    ½     pound elbow pasta, uncooked (!)
     2     tablespoons butter
     1     cup cottage cheese (not low fat)
     2     cups milk (not skim)
     1     teaspoon dry mustard
     1     pinch cayenne
     1     pinch nutmeg
     1     pound sharp or extra sharp
                    cheddar cheese, grated
 
SERVES: 4-6
ACTIVE TIME: 30 MIN
START TO FINISH: 1 ¼ HOURS
	Preheat oven to 375 degrees. Butter or grease a 9-inch round or square baking pan, or ovenproof bowl.
In a blender, puree the cottage cheese, milk, mustard, cayenne, nutmeg, ½ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon pepper together.
Reserve ¼ cup of the grated cheese for topping. In a large bowl, combine the remaining grated cheese, milk mixture, and uncooked pasta.
Pour into the prepared pan or ovenproof bowl, cover tightly with foil and bake 30 minutes. Uncover the pan, stir gently, sprinkle with the reserved cheese and dot with remaining tablespoon butter.
Bake, uncovered, 30 minutes more, until browned. Let cool 10-15 minutes before serving.
	Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees.
Add ziti to the boiling water and cook for 8-10 minutes, or until al dente; drain and rinse.
Meanwhile, butter or grease a 9-inch round or square baking pan or ovenproof bowl.
In a medium bowl, mix ziti, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, egg, tomato sauce, and salt (maybe garlic salt) and pepper to taste.
Pour the mixture into the pan. Top with the Parmesan cheese all around.
Bake, uncovered, for 30 minutes, until browned. Let cool 10-15 minutes before serving.
 Baked Ziti
     ½    pound ziti rigate (ridged)   
     1     cup part-skim ricotta
     1     egg, lightly beaten
     ¾    cup Parmesan cheese, grated
     1     cup part-skim mozzarella,
                    shredded
      1    jar (24-26 ounces) top-quality
                    tomato sauce (3 ½ cups)
