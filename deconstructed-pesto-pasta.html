Deconstructed Pesto Pasta

As good as regular Pesto Pasta is, sometimes it’s fun to mix things up a bit while still enjoying the same basic, but wonderful ingredients, in this case, garlic, oil, Parmesan cheese, basil, and pasta.

Deconstruction is usually more associated with sophisticated French philosophical discussions, but here the idea is simple: use all the Pesto Pasta ingredients, but without first blending them all together into a sauce. (It’s kind of like the Pompidou Museum in Paris, where the inside parts are all on the outside.)

This is especially nice for a relaxing Summer party or picnic.

Deconstructed Pesto Pasta

Chiffonade the basil leaves (say chiffonade slowly while working). To do this, stack several of the basil leaves together, roll them tightly along the length of the leaves, and then cut across the leaves in thin ribbons.

SERVES: 4-6
ACTIVE TIME: 30 MIN
START TO FINISH: 30 MIN

1 pound fusilli or radiatore pasta
6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
2 cups fresh basil leaves,
washed and dried
8 garlic cloves, minced
½ cup pinolas or pine nuts, or
shelled walnuts
1 cup grated Parmesan cheese,
or Parmesan/Romano
mixture

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
Meanwhile, toast the nuts in a skillet over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until golden in places, about 5 minutes. Pour onto a plate and set aside to cool.
When the water boils, add the pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente.
While the pasta is cooking, gently heat the olive oil and garlic over low heat in the same skillet used for the nuts, stirring frequently, until the garlic starts to turn golden, 4 to 8 minutes. Remove from the heat and stir in a teaspoon of salt and a 1/4 teaspoon of pepper.
When the pasta is done, scoop out a ½ cup of the cooking liquid and reserve. Drain the pasta and return it to the pot.
Pour the garlic and olive oil over the hot pasta and toss. Pour the reserved pasta water into the skillet, swirl it around, and pour it over the pasta. Toss well. Add the pine nuts and cheese and toss again until thoroughly mixed.
Transfer to a serving bowl and let cool for 5 minutes. Then, just before serving, toss in the basil. Pass extra freshly grated cheese around for those who want more.
