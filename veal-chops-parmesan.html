Veal Chops Parmesan

This recipe is from “60-Minute Gourmet” and is an example of how “great French cooking need not take hours and days of preparation to be brought to simple perfection.”

In French, it’s called Cotês de Veau Foyot, named after the greatest restaurant in Paris in the middle of the 19th century.

Today, this dish is still suitable for your most discriminating friends -- or family! – maybe with some noodles and a fresh tomato sauce.

Veal Chops With Parmesan

4 veal chops,
abut ¾ pound each
6 tablespoons butter, softened
½ cup breadcrumbs
½ cup Parmesan cheese
2 tablespoons shallots,
finely chopped
½ cup dry white wine


Fresh Tomato Sauce

2 tablespoons butter
¼ cup onion, finely chopped
1 garlic clove, finely minced
1 14-ounce can imported
tomatoes,
with tomato paste
1 bay leaf

Sprinkle the shallots over the bottom of the skillet in which the chops cooked and add the meat back into the pan.
Pour the wine around the chops.
Place, uncovered, in the oven and bake about 10 minutes until the tops are appetiz-ingly brown.
Place a layer of foil on top, letting it rest loosely over the veal. Return to the turned-off oven if you need to keep it warm.
When ready to serve, spoon the sauce around the veal.








If you’re making the noodles and tomato sauce, heat 1 tablespoon of butter in a saucepan and add the onion and garlic. Cook, stirring briefly.
Add the tomatoes, bay leaf, and a little salt and pepper.
Let simmer about 10 minutes.
Remove the bay leaf and swirl in the remaining butter (homogenize the sauce with an immersion blender if you’d like).
Add the hot sauce to the cooked noodles.

SERVES: 4
ACTIVE TIME: 30 MIN
START TO FINISH: 1 HOUR

Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.
Sprinkle the chops on both sides with salt and pepper.








Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in a heavy skillet and add the chops.
Cook over high heat until golden brown on one side, about 1-2 minutes. Turn the chops and cook 1-2 minutes longer or until golden brown on the other side.
Transfer the chops to a platter and keep warm.
Meanwhile, blend the breadcrumbs, cheese, and remaining 4 tablespoons of butter with your hands, until thoroughly worked together and blended.
Divide the mixture into four portions of equal size and work it into flat sheets to fit on top of each chop.
